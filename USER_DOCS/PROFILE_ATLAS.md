# Verbum Profile Atlas

This is the short identity guide for the four live Verbum profiles.

Use it when deciding where an idea belongs. This is not just a lore guide; it is a placement guide for design conversations.

---

## How to Read This Atlas

For each profile, pay attention to five things:

- **purpose** — why this profile exists
- **feels like** — the emotional and sensory lane
- **good verbs** — the kinds of play it naturally invites
- **good feature shapes** — the forms ideas often take here
- **red flags** — signs that the idea belongs somewhere else or is drifting badly

If a feature does not clearly fit at least two of those categories, keep talking before packetizing it.

---

## Veritas

### Purpose
The refined baseline: restrained, trustworthy, foundational, and clear.

### Feels like
Calm orientation, durable tools, legible systems, low-drama usefulness, modest craft, clean foundations.

### Good verbs
orient, teach, steady, restore, order, mark, carry, begin

### Good nouns / materials / imagery
primer, handbook, lamp, path, bell, timber, stone, margin, rule, table, vessel, field

### Good feature shapes
- foundational quality-of-life
- baseline systems that clarify rather than sprawl
- orientation texts and handbooks
- essential devotional or practical manuals
- core materials or practices that should exist everywhere

### Belongs here if...
- it should feel basic, durable, and widely appropriate
- it improves understanding or early play posture
- it would be strange for higher profiles to exist without it

### Red flags
- it only becomes interesting when paired with wonder-heavy expansion
- it is highly profile-flavored or luxury-like
- it starts pulling toward broad progression trees or specialized fantasy identity

### Canonical book forms
devotional primer, handbook, orientation text, essential field note

---

## Vocations

### Purpose
The domestic and vocational expansion above Veritas: work, craft, stewardship, place, ordinary beauty, and daily life.

### Feels like
Warm, local, seasonal, practical, artisanal, inhabited, restful without becoming empty.

### Good verbs
tend, mend, store, season, cultivate, build, bake, keep, gather, steward

### Good nouns / materials / imagery
garden, hearth, workshop, almanac, basket, linen, timber, pantry, field, kettle, fence, orchard, ash, hand tools

### Good feature shapes
- farming and garden systems
- household and workshop loops
- stewardship mechanics
- practical domestic items
- books about care, labor, season, and place
- player guidance that honors ordinary life and practical beauty

### Belongs here if...
- the center of gravity is home, work, rhythm, craft, or stewardship
- the player should feel rooted rather than dazzled
- the feature deepens ordinary life instead of broadening spectacle

### Red flags
- it becomes quest-heavy, combat-driven, or high-fantasy
- it wants giant tech or magic machinery to feel complete
- it is more about world conquest than household rhythm

### Canonical book forms
almanac, household guide, craft notebook, seasonal manual, kitchen or garden book

---

## Visions

### Purpose
The broad flagship line: exploration, wonder, layered progression, tech, magic, and larger systems.

### Feels like
Luminous, adventurous, learned, mythic, expansive, symbolically charged, full of discovery.

### Good verbs
seek, chart, decode, traverse, attune, reveal, assemble, discover, invoke, ascend

### Good nouns / materials / imagery
atlas, star, veil, compass, archive, relic, threshold, observatory, ruin, choir, sigil, current, gate

### Good feature shapes
- exploration systems
- high-magic and tech content
- layered progression families
- broad world-expanding mechanics
- atlases, compendia, chronicles, field guides
- manuals with symbolic resonance and real utility

### Belongs here if...
- breadth and wonder are part of the feature's center, not just decoration
- the player should feel drawn outward into discovery
- the system gains meaning from layered progression or larger-than-local scale

### Red flags
- it is only “Visions” because it sounds cool and big
- it breaks coherence in the name of more content
- it should actually be a simple Veritas foundation or a rooted Vocations loop

### Canonical book forms
atlas, chronicle, compendium, field guide, technical-mystical manual

---

## Vorago

### Purpose
The punitive pressure layer above Visions: the same world under harsher law, scarcity, attrition, and discipline.

### Feels like
Severe, weathered, austere, exposed, survivalist, disciplined, unforgiving but not nihilistic.

### Good verbs
endure, ration, brace, preserve, recover, fortify, withstand, adapt, watch, survive

### Good nouns / materials / imagery
ashes, rule, frost, ration, shelter, wound, drought, burden, ruin, salt, iron, hide, storm, hunger

### Good feature shapes
- pressure systems
- severe survival doctrine
- scarcity and exposure layers
- harsher laws over existing systems
- manuals of endurance and severe practical guidance

### Belongs here if...
- the feature's main function is to make the existing world bite harder
- the player should feel exposed, disciplined, and tested
- friction is the center of gravity, not merely an optional decoration

### Red flags
- it becomes cartoonishly evil or grimdark
- it reads like chaos for chaos's sake
- it introduces huge new wonder systems instead of pressure on existing ones

### Canonical book forms
rule, ash-book, severe handbook, survival doctrine, doctrine of endurance

## Ownership Questions

When you are unsure where an idea belongs, ask:

1. What kind of play does this invite most naturally?
2. Is its center of gravity foundational, domestic, expansive, or punitive?
3. Does the player mainly feel steadied, rooted, widened, or pressured?
4. Would the feature still make sense if all wonder-heavy expansion were removed?
5. Is the feature adding more *breadth* or more *law*?

Those five questions usually expose the true home pretty quickly.

---

## Upgrade Rule

Profiles inherit upward:

- Veritas flows into Vocations, Visions, and Vorago
- Vocations flows into Visions and Vorago
- Visions flows into Vorago
- Vorago-only content stays Vorago-only

This is why ownership should be decided early. Profile placement affects both feel and propagation.

## Common Misplacements

### “It is beautiful and mystical, so it must be Visions.”
Not necessarily. Some of the loveliest ideas belong to Vocations or even Veritas.

### “It is hard, so it must be Vorago.”
Not necessarily. Difficulty alone is not an identity. Vorago is about punitive law and survival pressure with disciplined tone.

### “It includes work, so it must be Vocations.”
Not necessarily. Work can appear in all profiles. The question is whether ordinary life and stewardship are the feature's center of gravity.

### “It touches everyone, so it should be Veritas.”
Only if it genuinely feels foundational and widely appropriate. Not all shared usefulness is baseline-worthy.
