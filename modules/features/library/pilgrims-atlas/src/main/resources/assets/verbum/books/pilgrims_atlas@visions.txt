Pilgrim's Atlas
A field manual for Visions.

Use this as orientation, not prophecy.
---PAGE---
Visions is the broad flagship line of Verbum.
It carries tech, magic, quests, progression, and long-range exploration in one luminous map.
Expect depth, not a single lane.
---PAGE---
Read the world by districts.
Keep one base for stability, one route for gathering, and one frontier for risk.
When a system opens, log what it feeds and what it unlocks.
---PAGE---
Prioritize durable loops:
power, transport, storage, and recovery.
Wonder matters, but infrastructure keeps wonder usable.
---PAGE---
Treat this atlas like a field guide.
Follow signs, test tools, and return with notes.
Visions rewards players who explore widely and build coherently.
