Pilgrim's Atlas
A field manual for Visions.

Use this as orientation, not prophecy.
---PAGE---
Visions is Verbum's flagship profile:
the full country of systems, wonder, and long roads.
It gathers tech, magic, quests, progression, and exploration into one living map.
---PAGE---
What Visions is for:
to give you many meaningful threads and let you braid them into one coherent life.
You are not meant to rush one branch and ignore the rest.
You are meant to learn a landscape.
---PAGE---
How it should feel:
luminous, learned, and exploratory.
You should feel like a builder-scholar on pilgrimage:
curious at the frontier, disciplined at home, always returning with better questions.
---PAGE---
How to begin well:
choose one anchor base for order,
one reliable route for supply,
and one frontier for risk and discovery.
When a new system opens, note what it consumes, what it produces, and what it unlocks.
---PAGE---
Prioritize durable loops:
power, transport, storage, recovery, and information.
Wonder is a gift, but infrastructure keeps wonder usable.
If your base cannot absorb new discoveries, your progress will fracture.
---PAGE---
What kind of play Visions invites:
wide exploration joined to coherent planning.
Test tools in the field, then bring them home and integrate them with care.
Visions rewards players who range far, document what they learn, and build systems that endure.
