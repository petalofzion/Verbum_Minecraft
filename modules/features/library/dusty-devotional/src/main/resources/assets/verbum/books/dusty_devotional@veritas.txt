Dusty Devotional
A manual for Veritas baseline.

This is a practical guide, not a quest line.
Use it to understand what Veritas is trying to protect.
---PAGE---
What Veritas is for:
to make Minecraft steadier, clearer, and easier to live in for a long time.

It is the baseline you can trust before any larger profile layers on top.
---PAGE---
How Veritas should feel:
plain, grounded, and reliable.

You should feel oriented, not overwhelmed.
Systems should read clearly and behave the same way tomorrow.
---PAGE---
What kind of play it invites:
slow improvement over sudden leaps.
Build places you can use every session.
Prefer workflows you can remember without notes.
---PAGE---
Pay attention to durable wins:
clear storage labels, walkable paths, modest farms, repair-friendly layouts,
and rooms that are easy to read at a glance.
---PAGE---
When deciding between two options, pick the one that is:
more readable,
easier to maintain,
and kinder to future you.

That is the Veritas posture: first things, done well, repeated calmly.
