Rule of Ashes

This is the manual for Vorago.
Vorago is for players who want pressure on purpose.
It keeps the breadth of Visions,
but removes easy recovery and loose play.
---PAGE---
Vorago should feel tight, exposed, and earned.
Small errors grow.
Delay has a cost.
Safety is built, not granted.
You are not meant to feel powerful by default.
---PAGE---
Play it as a discipline, not a sprint.
Plan each outing around food, fuel, shelter,
and a clean route home.
Take fewer risks, but take them deliberately.
Leave with a margin.
---PAGE---
Treat logistics as survival tools.
Reserves are progress.
Spare gear is progress.
A repaired base is progress.
If your supplies are thin, your next move is resupply.
---PAGE---
Expect attrition.
Weather, distance, darkness, and hunger are constant opponents.
Scout before committing.
Break contact early when a plan turns bad.
Live to return with information.
---PAGE---
Vorago invites patient, methodical play:
observe,
prepare,
execute,
recover,
repeat.
The world is the same world,
but it opens only to disciplined hands.
